# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences; assume good faith.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully — reviews here are about
  evidence and design, not about people.
* Keep discussion on topic: bugs, features, compatibility results, docs.
* Respect privacy: redact personal data from screenshots, logs and diagnostics
  before posting them. Never post someone else's data.
* Do not use the issue tracker for spam, promotion, or support for other tools.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.
* Encouraging or demonstrating bypassing of site permissions, paywalls,
  authentication or technical protection measures — this project will not accept
  such contributions, and they are off topic in its spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other project space, and to behaviour outside the project when it directly harms
participants.

## Reporting

Report abusive behaviour privately to the project owner through the repository's
contact channel (GitHub profile of the maintainer shown as the repository owner),
or via the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if no other
channel exists. The maintainer will review and respond as promptly as is
realistic for a volunteer project, and will keep reporter identity confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block accounts for
behaviour that violates this code, after a warning for a first, minor incident.
Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
